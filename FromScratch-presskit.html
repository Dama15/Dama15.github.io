From Scratch

From Scratch is a casual sandbox game where you can cut, fold, and paste cardboard to create any props you like. Choose from pre-made templates or design your own and start crafting!

Features

A cozy crafting simulator where you build custom cardboard props for local townspeople, bringing their stories to life one craft at a time.

Unique Selling Points

  • Creative Freedom — full creative control over each craft, from choosing templates to adding extra decorations
  • Custom Cutscenes — the end-of-order cutscene features the exact item the player made

Core Loop

  • Receive orders from NPCs, each with a short story told through photos and letters
  • Letters detail the requested craft before the crafting scene loads
  • Pick from a few order-related templates, then cut, fold, paste, and paint using tools like the box cutter and bone folder
  • Parcel and send the finished craft — the NPC replies with a thank-you note and photos of them using it
  • New orders can arrive mid-craft and queue up, so players choose what to work on next

Sandbox Mode

  • Skip the narrative and NPC orders entirely
  • Freely spawn any cardboard template or starter mesh to craft whatever you like

Cardboard Mesh System

  • Fully intuitive — no required order of operations
  • Spawn a template, then cut, crease, and paint in any sequence
  • Cut pieces remain fully functional cardboard meshes
