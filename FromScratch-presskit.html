From Scratch is a casual sandbox game where you can cut, fold, and paste cardboard to create any props you like. Choose from pre-made templates or design your own and start crafting!

Features

Core Loop

  • Take Orders: Read customers' orders and letters detailing requested crafts.
  • Craft: Cut, fold, paste, and paint using real crafting tools in any order.
  • Deliver: Send finished crafts to unlock thank-you notes, photos, and exciting rewards.
  • Manage incoming orders and grow your business.

Sandbox Mode (Extra Mode)

  • No Story Restrictions: Skip orders to freely spawn templates and craft anything.

Cardboard Mesh System

  • Non-Linear Crafting: Cut, crease, or paint in any sequence.
  • Cut pieces remain fully usable crafts.
